How to Use Categories

Our platform organizes resources into categories to help you find exactly what you need:

  • Project Documents: Reports, documentation, and guides for academic projects.
  • Interview Prep: Questions, answers, and tips for preparing for job interviews.
  • Useful Links: Curated links to external resources and learning platforms.
